# Literature (5 papers) — what each did, and what this project adds

| # | Paper | What they did | Relation to this repo |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gulamali F. et al. "Derivation, external and clinical validation of a deep learning approach for detecting intracranial hypertension." *npj Digital Medicine* 2024 (doi:10.1038/s41746-024-01227-0) | Deep learning biomarker for elevated ICP (aICP) from routinely collected extracranial waveforms; developed on MIMIC-III Waveform DB (N = 157 with ICP monitoring), external validation at Mount Sinai (N = 56), AUROC 0.80 external | Same open database and same question (ICP > threshold). We use **only finger PPG + ECG timing** and explainable features, and score with leave-one-patient-out on a much smaller set. |
| 2 | Nair S. et al. "A real-time deep learning approach for inferring intracranial pressure from routinely measured extracranial waveforms in the intensive care unit." medRxiv 2023 (doi:10.1101/2023.05.16.23289747); later *Comput Biol Med* 2024 | 600 h of ABP, ECG, PPG and ICP from 10 MIMIC-III patients, 10 s windows, six DL models; MAE ≈ 1.5 mmHg within patient, ≈ 5 mmHg multi-patient | Closest setup. We drop ABP (as a non-invasive device would: no arterial line), use 60 s windows, and report only held-out-patient numbers next to training numbers. |
| 3 | Bradley G. R. E., Kyriacou P. A. Near-infrared PPG features vs ICP (27 patients, intraparenchymal probe), *Biomedical Signal Processing and Control* 98:106759, 2024; and classical ML for NIR-PPG ICP, *BSPC* 96B:106517, 2024 | Cerebral (forehead) NIR PPG features that change with ICP; classical ML estimation | The physiological feature idea (pulse shape → ICP) we copy, but on **finger** PPG, which is further from the brain. Their data is not open. |
| 4 | Hu X., Xu P., Scalzo F., Vespa P., Bergsneider M. "Morphological clustering and analysis of continuous intracranial pressure" (MOCAIP). *IEEE Trans Biomed Eng* 56(3):696–705, 2009 (PMC2673331) | ECG QRS detection + beat clustering + sub-peak designation on the ICP pulse; 128 morphology metrics | Our beat pipeline (R-peak anchoring, template-based beat quality, per-beat landmarks) follows the same pattern, applied to the PPG pulse instead of the ICP pulse. |
| 5 | Liu X. et al. "Continuous monitoring of cerebrovascular reactivity through pulse transit time and intracranial pressure." *Physiological Measurement* 2019 (PMID 30577032, PMC7197410) | 45 SAH patients; PTT = ECG R-wave → SpO2 pulse onset; PTT slow waves correlated with ICP; beat-to-beat PTT negatively related to arterial pressure | Same PTT definition (R-peak → PPG foot). We test PTT as a direct predictor of ICP level and hypertension across held-out patients, not as a reactivity index. |

## What this repo extends
- Held-out-patient scoring only (leave-one-patient-out), with training numbers beside it, so overfitting to patients is visible.
- PPG + ECG only, no arterial line, explainable features with a stated physiological reason, plus a tiny CNN for comparison.
- Fully open, reproducible from PhysioNet with `make reproduce`.
